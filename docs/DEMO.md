# Broker demonstration

The Stocklana demonstration is a personal stock broker you can call, and that calls you when a saved watch condition occurs. It researches stocks, discusses your portfolio, carries out authorized Buy and Sell instructions, and leaves results you can reopen. Phone connects to your continuing Primary Dexter through the shared OpenDexter tools.

## Complete demonstration

1. Show the existing account, linked number, trading permissions and saved watch. Explain where the person can manage them.
2. Call Dexter and discuss a stock using current research and account data. For a portfolio overview, give the useful summary first, including any incomplete coverage. Ask for a position or movement breakdown to exercise detail on request. A specific stock question should receive that stock's answer first.
3. Complete an explicitly authorized Buy and Sell. Hear each actual outcome and reopen its saved result. A prepared quote alone does not establish execution.
4. Let a saved watch condition trigger a call. Dexter explains the event and its observation time, follows the purpose of the call, and handles a relevant question or authorized instruction. An operator manually placing a call does not prove the watch trigger.
5. Continue the conversation naturally, including an interruption and a second request while work is pending. Confirm that neither the original answer nor the new instruction disappears, and that accepted work remains recoverable after the call.

Core continuity, accepted work, results, receipts, current authority and saved watch instructions are required. Richer provider-backed recall is off the mandatory hackathon path. Include it only if X's separate recall, correction and forgetting qualification finishes in time. A provider plan or captured transcript alone does not prove that feature.

The preliminary portfolio test below is an initial check. Product acceptance requires the complete useful conversation and stock journey above. Record the finished journey for the demo video; prepare the entry, judge access and submission confirmation alongside it.
